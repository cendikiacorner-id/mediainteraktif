UNIT 1

My Digital World

Talk about digital devices and online activities.

MY DIGITAL DAY

Hi, I'm Nadia. Like most teenagers, I use digital devices every day. I have a smartphone, and my family has a laptop and a tablet at home.

In the morning, I check the school group chat to see today's schedule. During class, we sometimes use the laptop to search for information or watch learning videos. Our teacher also shares materials through an online classroom.

In the evening, I usually upload my homework, chat with my friends, and watch videos. On weekends, I like to browse recipes and learn simple English songs online.

Digital technology really helps me learn faster. However, I try to limit my screen time to two hours a day, because too much screen time is not good for my eyes and my sleep.

UNIT 2

Are You Digitally Safe?

Give advice about digital safety.

STAY SAFE ONLINE

The internet is useful, but it can also be dangerous if we are not careful. Here are some tips to stay safe online.

First, you should use a strong password. A strong password has letters, numbers, and symbols. You shouldn't use your birthday or your name as a password.

Second, you must not share your personal information. Never give your address, phone number, or school name to strangers online.

Third, you should think before you click. If you receive a strange link, don't open it. It may contain a virus or a scam.

Fourth, you had better check the information before you share it. Many hoaxes spread quickly because people share them without checking.

Remember: if something looks too good to be true, it probably is. Stay smart and stay safe!

🔤 Grammar Focus: Modals of Advice & Obligation

UNIT 3

Netiquette

Good manners in online communication.

NETIQUETTE: BE KIND ONLINE

Netiquette comes from the words network and etiquette. It means good manners when we communicate on the internet.

When you send a message to your teacher, always use polite language. Start with a greeting such as "Good morning, Ma'am" and introduce yourself. Don't send messages late at night.

Avoid typing in CAPITAL LETTERS, because it looks like shouting. Also, don't spread hoaxes or unkind comments. If you post something rude, you will hurt other people's feelings.

If you disagree with someone, you should respond politely. Remember that there is a real person behind every screen. Kindness costs nothing, even online.

Finally, always respect other people's privacy. If you want to share someone's photo, you must ask for permission first.

📝 Vocabulary

🔤 Grammar Focus: Conditional Sentence Type 1

Digunakan untuk kemungkinan nyata di masa depan.
RumusIf + Simple Present, ... will + Verb 1
If you post something rude, you will hurt other people's feelings.
If you use a weak password, your account will be hacked.
You will get a virus if you open that strange link. (urutan boleh dibalik)
⚠️ Setelah if memakai present tense, BUKAN will.
❌ If you will click ...  →  ✅ If you click ...
