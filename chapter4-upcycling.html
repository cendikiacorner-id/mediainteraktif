UNIT 1

Look At My New Creation

Describe a product made from used materials.

MY UPCYCLED PENCIL HOLDER

Look at my new creation! This is a pencil holder made from a used tin can. I made it in my art class last week. It looks colourful and cute.

The pencil holder is about fifteen centimetres tall. It is made of a used milk can, and it is covered with recycled paper and a piece of ribbon. I painted it blue and yellow. I also added some flower stickers on the surface.

This pencil holder is very useful. It can hold pens, pencils, rulers, and scissors. It keeps my study table tidy. The best part is that it is cheap and eco-friendly, because I only used materials that were going to be thrown away.

By upcycling, we can reduce waste and save money. We also help protect our environment. Why don't you make one too?

UNIT 2

They're On Sale

Promote a product and talk about prices.

AT THE SCHOOL BAZAAR

Buyer: Excuse me, how much is this tote bag?

Seller: It's twenty-five thousand rupiah. It's on sale today!

Buyer: Wow, that's cheap. What is it made from?

Seller: It's made from used jeans. My friends and I made it ourselves. It's strong and eco-friendly.

Buyer: How much are these pencil holders?

Seller: They're ten thousand rupiah each. If you buy two, you'll get a discount of twenty percent.

Buyer: That sounds great! Can I get a lower price for the tote bag?

Seller: Hmm, how about twenty-two thousand? That's my best price.

Buyer: Deal! I'll take one tote bag and two pencil holders, please.

📝 Vocabulary

🔤 Grammar Focus: Asking & Telling Prices

How much is this bag? → It's Rp25,000.
HOW MUCH AREuntuk benda jamak

each = per buah  •  a pair = sepasang  •  a dozen = selusin

UNIT 3

How to Make It

Write a procedure text — how to make something from used materials.

HOW TO MAKE A PENCIL HOLDER FROM A USED TIN CAN

GOAL To make a colourful pencil holder from a used tin can.

MATERIALS

  • a used tin can
  • coloured paper
  • glue and scissors
  • paint and a brush
  • a ribbon and some stickers

STEPS

First, wash the tin can and dry it well.
Second, remove the label carefully.
Next, paint the outside of the can and let it dry.
After that, cut the coloured paper and glue it around the can.
Then, tie the ribbon around the middle of the can.
Finally, decorate it with stickers. Your pencil holder is ready to use!

Note: Be careful with the sharp edge of the can. Ask an adult for help if necessary.

📝 Vocabulary

🗂️ Structure of a Procedure Text

PartIsi
1. Goal / AimTujuan — biasanya judul: How to make ...
2. Materials / ToolsDaftar bahan dan alat yang diperlukan
3. StepsLangkah-langkah berurutan menggunakan kalimat perintah

🔤 Grammar Focus: Imperative Sentences

Imperative = kalimat perintah. Dimulai langsung dengan Verb 1, tanpa subjek.
PositifWash the can. / Cut the paper. / Glue it around the can.
NegatifDon't touch the sharp edge. / Do not use too much glue.
SopanPlease wash the can. / Wash the can, please.
❌ You wash the can.  →  ✅ Wash the can.
