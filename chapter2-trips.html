UNIT 1

Going to a National Park

Talk about past experience — asking and answering about a trip.

Galang: Hi, Sinta! You look so happy. Where did you go last weekend?

Sinta: I went to Baluran National Park with my family.

Galang: Really? When did you go there?

Sinta: We went there on Saturday morning and came back on Sunday evening.

Galang: How did you get there?

Sinta: We drove there by car. It took about four hours from Mojokerto.

Galang: Did you see any wild animals?

Sinta: Yes, we did! We saw deer, buffaloes, and many birds. But we didn't see any peacocks because it was too hot.

Galang: Wow, amazing! Did you bring binoculars?

Sinta: No, we didn't. We only brought a camera. Next time you should come with us!

📝 Vocabulary

🔤 Grammar Focus: Simple Past Tense

1. Positive (+) → Subject + Verb 2
We went to Baluran. / I saw a deer. / They brought a camera.
2. Negative (−) → Subject + did not (didn't) + Verb 1 (kembali ke bentuk dasar!)
We didn't see any peacocks. (bukan: didn't saw)
3. Interrogative (?) → Did + Subject + Verb 1 ?
— Did you bring binoculars?  →  Short answer: Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
4. WH-question
Question wordExample
WhereWhere did you go last weekend?
WhenWhen did you go to the national park?
HowHow did you get there?
Who withWho did you go with?
⚠️ Ingat: setelah did / didn't, kata kerja SELALU bentuk dasar (Verb 1).
✅ Did you go?    ❌ Did you went?

UNIT 2

What Did You Do There?

Identify the parts of a recount text.

MY HOLIDAY IN MALANG

[Orientation] Last holiday, my family and I went to Malang, East Java. We stayed there for three days. We travelled by train from Mojokerto.

[Events] Once we got there, we were taken straight to the hotel. After that, we had lunch at a small restaurant near the hotel.

On the second day, we visited Jatim Park. We rode a roller coaster and watched an animal show. My little brother was very excited. In the afternoon, we bought some souvenirs for our friends.

On the last day, we went to Coban Rondo Waterfall. The water was cold and clear. We took a lot of photos there. Finally, we went back home in the evening.

[Reorientation] It was a wonderful holiday. I really enjoyed the trip and I hope I can visit Malang again someday.

📝 Vocabulary

🗂️ Structure of a Recount Text

PartIsi
1. OrientationPembuka: siapa, kapan, di mana, dengan siapa. Contoh: "Last holiday, my family and I went to Malang."
2. EventsRangkaian kejadian secara urut (hari 1, hari 2, dst).
3. ReorientationPenutup: kesan/perasaan penulis. Contoh: "It was a wonderful holiday."

🔗 Time Connectives (penanda urutan waktu)

AwalTengahAkhir
First, At first, Once we got there, Last holidayThen, After that, Next, On the second day, Later, In the afternoonFinally, At last, In the end, On the last day
Once we got there, we were taken straight to the hotel.
📌 Ciri bahasa recount: past tense, kata ganti orang pertama (I / we), time connectives, dan action verbs (went, visited, rode, bought).

    UNIT 3

    My Underwater Adventure

    Write a recount text about your experience.

    MY FIRST SNORKELLING

    Last July, I went to Karimunjawa Island with my cousins. It was my first time snorkelling, so I felt nervous but excited.

    In the morning, the guide gave us a mask, a snorkel, and fins. While I was putting on the fins, my cousin jumped into the water. The sea was calm and very clear.

    While I was swimming above the coral reef, I saw two sea turtles. They were swimming slowly under me. I also saw many colourful fish. When the guide called us, we were taking photos underwater.

    In the afternoon, we had grilled fish on the beach. I was very tired but very happy. It was the best experience of my holiday.

    📝 Vocabulary

    🔤 Grammar Focus: Past Continuous + When / While

    Past Continuous → Subject + was / were + Verb-ing
    dipakai untuk kejadian yang sedang berlangsung di masa lampau.
    WASI, He, She, It
    WEREYou, We, They
    I was swimming. / They were taking photos.
    Menggabungkan dua kejadian:
    WHILEdiikuti kejadian panjang (past continuous)
    WHENdiikuti kejadian pendek/tiba-tiba (simple past)
    While I was swimming, I saw the sea turtles.
    I was swimming when I saw the sea turtles. (arti sama, urutan dibalik)
    When the guide called us, we were taking photos.
    💡 Trik cepat: While + V-ing (kegiatan lama)  •  When + V2 (kejadian sekejap yang memotong).
