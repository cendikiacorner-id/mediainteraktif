CHAPTER 1 · UNIT 3 · INDONESIAN BIRDS

Active & Passive
Voice

Tujuan hari ini:
1. Membedakan kalimat aktif dan pasif
2. Mengubah aktif menjadi pasif
3. Menggunakan pasif untuk membahas satwa Indonesia

Apa bedanya?

ACTIVE
Hunters kill the hornbills.
Fokus pada PELAKU
Siapa yang melakukan?
PASSIVE
The hornbills are killed by hunters.
Fokus pada KORBAN / OBJEK
Apa yang terjadi?
Artinya sama. Yang berbeda hanya apa yang ditonjolkan.

Rumus Dasar

S + be + V3 ( + by + pelaku )
BagianPenjelasan
SObjek kalimat aktif, pindah ke depan
beis / am / are / was / were / been — menyesuaikan tense
V3Kata kerja bentuk ketiga (past participle)
byPelaku — boleh dihilangkan jika tidak penting

3 Langkah Mengubah Aktif → Pasif

AKTIF:   People   protect   the bekantan.
Subjek      Kata kerja      Objek
1️⃣ Objek the bekantan → pindah ke depan jadi subjek
2️⃣ Tambahkan be sesuai tense → present, subjek tunggal → is
3️⃣ Ubah protect → V3 → protected
⬇
PASIF: The bekantan is protected by people.

Bentuk "be" Menurut Tense

TenseAktifPasif
Simple PresentThey hunt the birds.The birds are hunted.
Simple PastThey hunted the birds.The birds were hunted.
Present PerfectThey have hunted it.It has been hunted.
Simple FutureThey will hunt it.It will be hunted.
ModalThey must protect it.It must be protected.
Kuncinya: V3 tidak pernah berubah. Yang berubah hanya bentuk be-nya.

Kapan Pakai Pasif?

  • Pelaku tidak diketahui
    The nest was destroyed. (tidak tahu siapa)
  • Pelaku tidak penting
    Bekantan is found only in Borneo.
  • Yang penting objeknya
    The helmeted hornbill is threatened by poaching.
  • Teks ilmiah / laporan
    Sering dipakai di report text tentang hewan.

Latihan 1 — Ubah ke Pasif

1. People call this bird "rangkong gading".
This bird is called "rangkong gading".
2. Hunters killed many hornbills last year.
Many hornbills were killed by hunters last year.
3. The government protects the bekantan.
The bekantan is protected by the government.
4. Farmers destroyed the mangrove forest.
The mangrove forest was destroyed by farmers.

Latihan 2 — Lengkapi

1. Orangutans _____ (find) in Sumatra and Borneo.
are found
2. Many trees _____ (cut) down last month.
were cut
3. This species _____ (must / protect) by everyone.
must be protected
4. A new nest _____ (build) by the birds every year.
is built
5. The eggs _____ (eat) by a snake yesterday.
were eaten

Ringkasan

S + be + V3 + ( by pelaku )
✅ Objek aktif → jadi subjek pasif
✅ be menyesuaikan tense
✅ Kata kerja selalu V3
✅ by boleh dihilangkan
✅ Banyak dipakai dalam report text tentang satwa
