UNIT 1

Andre and Princess Suripit

Identify the plot and the characters of a fantasy story.

ANDRE AND PRINCESS SURIPIT

ORIENTATION Once upon a time, there was a poor but kind boy named Andre. He lived with his mother in a small village near a big forest. Every morning he went to the forest to collect firewood.

COMPLICATION One day, while Andre was collecting firewood, he heard a tiny voice crying for help. He looked around and found a very small princess trapped under a fallen branch. "Please help me!" she said. Andre lifted the branch carefully and set her free. The princess said, "Thank you, kind boy. My name is Princess Suripit. I will give you a magic seed."

However, when Andre came home, a greedy man from the village stole the magic seed. Suddenly, the sky turned dark and a huge storm came. The greedy man was very frightened.

RESOLUTION Andre ran back to the forest and asked Princess Suripit for help. She waved her tiny hand and the storm stopped immediately. The greedy man returned the seed and said sorry. Andre planted the seed and it grew into a golden tree. Finally, Andre and his mother lived happily and shared the golden fruits with all the villagers.

Moral value: Kindness always comes back to us.

📝 Vocabulary

🗂️ Generic Structure of a Narrative Text

PartIsi
1. OrientationPengenalan tokoh, waktu, dan tempat. Sering diawali "Once upon a time...", "Long time ago...".
2. ComplicationMunculnya masalah/konflik yang dihadapi tokoh utama.
3. ResolutionMasalah terselesaikan (happy/sad ending).
4. Coda / Moral valuePesan moral cerita (opsional).

UNIT 2

Back to the 90s

A time-travel fantasy story — direct and indirect speech.

BACK TO THE 90s

ORIENTATION Last month, Rani found an old cassette player in her grandmother's storeroom. It was dusty and looked very old. "What a strange machine!" she said.

COMPLICATION While Rani was cleaning the machine, she accidentally pressed the red button. Suddenly, a bright light appeared and she fell into a swirling portal. When she opened her eyes, she was standing on a street full of old cars and people wearing strange clothes. A boy on a bicycle stopped and asked, "Are you lost?" Rani replied, "What year is this?" The boy laughed and answered, "It is 1995, of course!"

Rani was shocked. She had no phone signal and she did not know how to go home. She walked around the town nervously until she found the same cassette player in a small shop.

RESOLUTION Bravely, Rani pressed the red button again. The bright light appeared once more and she woke up in her grandmother's storeroom. Everything was back to normal. She told her grandmother about the adventure, but her grandmother only smiled and said that the 90s were indeed a wonderful time.

📝 Vocabulary

🔤 Grammar Focus: Direct & Indirect Speech

Direct speech = kalimat langsung, memakai tanda kutip " ".
Indirect speech = kalimat tak langsung, tanpa tanda kutip, kata kerja mundur satu tense.
DirectIndirect
He said, "It is 1995."He said that it was 1995.
She said, "I am lost."She said that she was lost.
He said, "I go to school."He said that he went to school.
She said, "I will help you."She said that she would help me.
Perubahan kata: am/is → was • are → were • V1 → V2 • will → would • can → could • today → that day • here → there

🔗 Conjunctions in Narrative

Urutan waktuSebab-akibatPertentangan
then, after that, suddenly, finally, meanwhilebecause, so, thereforebut, however, although
Suddenly, a bright light appeared. / She was shocked because she had no phone signal. / However, a greedy man stole the seed.

UNIT 3

The Multiverse Story of Timun Mas

Adverbs of manner & writing your own fantasy story.

TIMUN MAS

ORIENTATION Long time ago, in a small village in Central Java, there lived a widow named Mbok Srini. She lived alone and really wanted a child. One day, a green giant gave her a magic cucumber seed. "Plant this seed," he said loudly, "but you must give me the child when she is seventeen."

COMPLICATION Mbok Srini planted the seed and it grew into a golden cucumber. Inside it, she found a beautiful baby girl and named her Timun Mas. Years passed quickly. When Timun Mas was seventeen, the giant came back angrily to take her. Mbok Srini gave Timun Mas four magic bags and told her to run fast.

Timun Mas ran quickly into the forest. The giant chased her furiously. She threw the first bag of cucumber seeds and they grew into a wide cucumber field. Then she threw needles, and they became a thick bamboo forest. Next, she threw salt and it became a wide sea.

RESOLUTION Finally, Timun Mas threw the last bag, which contained shrimp paste. It turned into a boiling mud lake. The giant fell into it and drowned. Timun Mas walked home safely and lived happily with her mother.

Moral value: Never give up, and be brave in facing your problems.

📝 Vocabulary

🔤 Grammar Focus: Adverbs of Manner

Adverb of manner menjelaskan bagaimana suatu perbuatan dilakukan. Biasanya dibentuk dari adjective + -ly dan diletakkan setelah kata kerja.
AdjectiveAdverbAdjectiveAdverb
quickquicklyangryangrily
happyhappilycarefulcarefully
bravebravelysafesafely
loudloudlynervousnervously
Timun Mas ran quickly into the forest. (bagaimana ia berlari?)
⚠️ Irregular: good → well • fast → fast • hard → hard (tidak pakai -ly)
