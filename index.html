1. What Is Passive Voice?

Passive voice focuses on the action, not the doer.
Passive voice berfokus pada tindakan, bukan pada pelakunya.
We use passive voice when the action is more important than who does it.
Kita menggunakan passive voice ketika tindakan lebih penting daripada siapa yang melakukannya.
Example / Contoh: The classroom is cleaned every day.
Active: The students clean the classroom every day.
Passive: The classroom is cleaned by the students every day.

2. Passive Voice Patterns

Simple Present Passive

Subject + am / is / are + Verb 3 (+ by ...)
The classroom is cleaned every day.
Kelas dibersihkan setiap hari.
English is studied by many students.
Bahasa Inggris dipelajari oleh banyak siswa.

Simple Past Passive

Subject + was / were + Verb 3 (+ by ...)
The cake was eaten by Rina yesterday.
Kue itu dimakan oleh Rina kemarin.
The letter was written by the teacher.
Surat itu ditulis oleh guru.

3. How to Change Active into Passive

Step 1: Find the object.
Langkah 1: Cari objek pada kalimat aktif.
Step 2: Put the object in front.
Langkah 2: Pindahkan objek ke awal kalimat.
Step 3: Choose the correct be.
Langkah 3: Pilih be yang sesuai.
Step 4: Change the verb into Verb 3.
Langkah 4: Ubah kata kerja menjadi Verb 3.
Step 5: Add by + doer if needed.
Langkah 5: Tambahkan by + pelaku jika perlu.
Example / Contoh: Rina wrote the letter → The letter was written by Rina.
